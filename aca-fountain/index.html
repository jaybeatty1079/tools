The Payroll Fountain

Plan tier

  • Trigger event: the monthly wellness activity that triggers the reward.
  • Wellness Reward: paid from the plan's fixed indemnity benefit, post-tax, on the same paycheck. Its own line, not the company's.
  • Plan Dollars: the employee's withholding, toward the benefits.

Where the numbers come from

Illustrative only. Every employee's number is different. No specific tax outcome is guaranteed; results depend on a payroll analysis and on current federal and state law. Figures leave out any workers' compensation, FUTA or SUTA effect. Reward payments in excess of unreimbursed medical expenses may be taxable to the employee.
