# Biologics Atlas

Live: https://jaybeatty1079.github.io/tools/biologics-atlas/

A catalogue of orthobiologics by what the surgeon is treating, with the honest state of the evidence next to every product: 85 surgical applications, 369 products, every FDA number checked against openFDA, shown on a 3D skeleton.

- `index.html` is the whole app; `skeleton.glb` is the 3D model it loads.
- `data/` is the product, application, market and FDA-check data. `research/` is the research record behind it.
- `RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` and `SCHEMA.md` explain the data. `MODEL-ATTRIBUTION.md` and `MODEL-LICENSE.md` credit the skeleton (BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy).

Educational reference built from public sources. Not medical or regulatory advice.
