# Dealer City

Live: https://jaybeatty1079.github.io/tools/bonus-lot/

A Section 125 wellness plan explained to a dealership general manager as a game: build the group as a night city in WebGL (three.js), run the payroll, set the pay plan, and watch what the plan adds to store net. Illustrative only; not affiliated with any video game or its publisher.

- `index.html` is the engine, `sectors.js` the per-industry content, `model.js` the math and the share-link validator. `SECTORS.md` says how to add an industry.
- `test.mjs` checks the model, the opening copy and the banned claims: `node test.mjs` (Node 18+, no dependencies).
- `models/` holds the cars (Kenney Car Kit, CC0) with their licence.
- No dollar figure anywhere belongs to an individual employee. No real group or client is named.
