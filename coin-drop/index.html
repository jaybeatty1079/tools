The payroll coin drop

  • Trigger event: the activity that triggers the reward each month.
  • The reward comes from Pulse but is integrated in payroll for efficiency.

Where the numbers come from

One paycheck is the sample from the Pulse deck: married, joint filer, $65,000 a year, California, at the $1,200 level, scaled from weekly to a month, plus the payroll tax the company pays on it. The whole company is that same paycheck for every employee, with the share who qualify enrolled. For each enrolled employee, $1,200 is withheld before tax into Pulse Section 125. The monthly trigger event pays a $1,000 Wellness Reward into the employee's account; the reward is paid after tax, comes from Pulse and runs through payroll. The other $200 is Pulse Plan $, toward the benefits and the claims. Because that $1,200 is withheld before tax, payroll taxes fall on both sides: the employee takes home about $79 more, and the company stops paying $91.80, pays its $35 Pulse admin fee out of that, and keeps $56.80. Health insurance and the 401(k) do not change.

Illustrative only. Every employee's number is different. No specific tax outcome is guaranteed; results depend on a payroll analysis and on current federal and state law. Figures leave out any workers' compensation, FUTA or SUTA effect.
