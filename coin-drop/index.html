Where the numbers come from

One paycheck is an illustrative employee at a smaller company: single, no withholding adjustments (0/0), $40,000 a year, paid weekly, California, at the $1,200 level. Withholding is worked out from the 2026 federal and California tables and scaled to a month, plus the payroll tax the company pays on it. A $160 a month health premium and a 1% 401(k) contribution are illustrative. The whole company is that same paycheck for every employee, with the share who qualify enrolled. For each enrolled employee, $1,200 is withheld before tax into Section 125. The monthly trigger event pays a $1,000 Wellness Reward into the employee's account; the reward is paid after tax, comes from the plan and runs through payroll. The other $200 is Plan $, toward the benefits and the claims. Because that $1,200 is withheld before tax, payroll taxes fall on both sides: the employee takes home about $88 more, and the company stops paying $91.80, pays its $35 admin fee out of that, and keeps $56.80. Health insurance and the 401(k) do not change.

Illustrative only. Every employee's number is different. No specific tax outcome is guaranteed; results depend on a payroll analysis and on current federal and state law. Figures leave out any workers' compensation, FUTA or SUTA effect.
